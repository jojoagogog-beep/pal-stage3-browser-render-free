# PAL Product Image Alt Guard — App Store submission draft

- App name: PAL Product Image Alt Guard
- Price: US$4.99/month
- Trial: 7 days
- Scope: read_products
- Protected customer data: not used

## Introduction
Find product images with missing or repeated alt text.

## Details
PAL Product Image Alt Guard performs a read-only product catalog audit, shows matching product records, links to Shopify Admin, and exports a diagnostic CSV. It never edits products.

## Reviewer steps
1. Install and approve read_products.
2. Open the app and run the audit.
3. Review the sample condition in /review-demo.
4. Export CSV and confirm product links.
5. Confirm no product data is changed.
